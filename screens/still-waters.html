Why Still Waters

Some questions surface
before noon.

Still Waters is the shortest container we offer, and the setting is deliberate. Pinecrest Lake is still, clear, and undramatic — a surface that shows you exactly what is placed in front of it, nothing more, nothing less.

In four hours, beginning at 10am, the session moves directly into a deep enquiry into the true nature of reality. A light lunch by the water closes the morning's work before you return to the rest of your day.

10:00am

Arrival at the lake

A short opening at the water's edge to name what is actually unresolved, so the enquiry begins at the point rather than circling toward it.

Midday

Direct enquiry

Dialogue and silence beside the still water, given fully to a deep enquiry into the true nature of reality.

2:00pm

Light lunch & departure

A light lunch by the lake closes the session. You leave with a clearer view of what was seen, and whether a deeper container is called for.

The Right Fit

A first taste.
Not a lesser version.

This is for someone who can give a single morning to the question — whether as a complete session in itself, or before deciding if the work asks for more time. Prior meditation experience is not required. Honesty and intellectual seriousness are.

Which Container?

Choose Still Waters when a single morning is what your schedule or your question allows. Choose one day when you are ready to give the inquiry your full attention. Choose the retreat when you need distance from ordinary life entirely.
